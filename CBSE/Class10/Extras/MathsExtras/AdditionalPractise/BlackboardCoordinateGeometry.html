QUESTION 01 Distance Formula Fundamentals CBSE Board Core
Find the distance between the points \( A(2, 3) \) and \( B(4, 1) \).
Given Points: \( A(x_1=2, y_1=3), B(x_2=4, y_2=1) \)
01 / 50
Detailed Solution 01
Distance Formula:
\( d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2} \)
\( d = \sqrt{(4 - 2)^2 + (1 - 3)^2} = \sqrt{2^2 + (-2)^2} = \sqrt{4 + 4} = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}\text{ units} \).
QUESTION 02 Collinearity of Three Points CBSE Conceptual
Determine if the points \( (1, 5), (2, 3) \) and \( (-2, -11) \) are collinear using the distance formula.
Given Points: \( A(1, 5), B(2, 3), C(-2, -11) \)
02 / 50
Detailed Solution 02
Calculate Distances:
\( AB = \sqrt{(2-1)^2 + (3-5)^2} = \sqrt{1 + 4} = \sqrt{5} \)
\( BC = \sqrt{(-2-2)^2 + (-11-3)^2} = \sqrt{16 + 196} = \sqrt{212} = 2\sqrt{53} \)
\( AC = \sqrt{(-2-1)^2 + (-11-5)^2} = \sqrt{9 + 256} = \sqrt{265} \)

Since \( AB + BC \neq AC \), the points are not collinear.
QUESTION 03 Equidistant Point on X-axis CBSE Board Standard
Find the point on the x-axis which is equidistant from \( (2, -5) \) and \( (-2, 9) \).
Let required point be \( P(x, 0) \). Given points: \( A(2, -5), B(-2, 9) \)
03 / 50
Detailed Solution 03
Equidistant Condition: \( PA = PB \implies PA^2 = PB^2 \)

\( (x - 2)^2 + (0 - (-5))^2 = (x - (-2))^2 + (0 - 9)^2 \)
\( x^2 - 4x + 4 + 25 = x^2 + 4x + 4 + 81 \)
\( -4x + 29 = 4x + 85 \implies -8x = 56 \implies x = -7 \)

Required point is (-7, 0).
