QUESTION 01 Electric Current & Charge CBSE Board Core
A conductor carries a charge of \( Q = 1.6 \text{ C} \) passing through a cross-section in \( t = 4 \text{ s} \). Calculate:
(a) The electric current (\(I\)) in the conductor.
(b) The total number of electrons (\(n\)) passing through the cross-section (Given \(e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}\)).
Given: \( Q = 1.6\text{ C} \), \( t = 4\text{ s} \), \( e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C} \)
01 / 50
Detailed Solution 01
(a) Electric Current (\(I\)):
\( I = \frac{Q}{t} = \frac{1.6}{4} = 0.4\text{ A} = 400\text{ mA} \)

(b) Number of Electrons (\(n\)):
\( Q = ne \implies n = \frac{Q}{e} = \frac{1.6}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}} = 1.0 \times 10^{19}\text{ electrons} \)

QUESTION 17Wire Stretching ProblemCBSE High Yield
