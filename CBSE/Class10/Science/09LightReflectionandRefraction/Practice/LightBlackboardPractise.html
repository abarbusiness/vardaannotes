📜 Laws of Reflection

1. Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection: \( \angle i = \angle r \)

🪞 Plane Mirror Image Properties

• Virtual, erect, same size (\( m = +1 \))
• Image distance = Object distance (\( v = -u \))
• Laterally inverted  |  Focal length \( f = \infty \)

SPHERICAL MIRRORS — FORMULAS, RAY RULES & SIGN CONVENTION

🔢 Mirror Formula & Magnification

\( \dfrac{1}{v} + \dfrac{1}{u} = \dfrac{1}{f} \)  |  \( f = \dfrac{R}{2} \)
\( m = -\dfrac{v}{u} = \dfrac{h'}{h} \)
m < 0 → Real/Inverted | m > 0 → Virtual/Erect

📋 Sign Convention Rules

• Object distance \( u \): always −ve
• Concave mirror: \( f = -\text{ve} \), \( R = -\text{ve} \)
• Convex mirror: \( f = +\text{ve} \), \( R = +\text{ve} \)

REFRACTION OF LIGHT & SNELL'S LAW

⚡ Snell's Law & Refractive Index

\( n_1 \sin i = n_2 \sin r \)
\( n = \dfrac{c}{v} = \dfrac{\lambda_{\text{air}}}{\lambda_{\text{medium}}} \)
Relative: \( {}_{1}n_{2} = \dfrac{n_2}{n_1} = \dfrac{v_1}{v_2} \)

📑 Refraction Through Glass Slab

• Emergent ray parallel to incident ray (\( i = e \))
• Apparent Depth: \( \text{Apparent Depth} = \dfrac{\text{Real Depth}}{n} \)
• Lateral Shift \( \Delta y = t \left(1 - \dfrac{1}{n}\right) \)

SPHERICAL LENSES — LENS FORMULA & MAGNIFICATION

🔢 Lens Formula & Magnification

\( \dfrac{1}{v} - \dfrac{1}{u} = \dfrac{1}{f} \)
\( m = \dfrac{v}{u} = \dfrac{h'}{h} \)
Convex lens: \( f = +\text{ve} \) | Concave lens: \( f = -\text{ve} \)

🔍 Lens Image Characteristics

• Convex Lens: Real/Virtual depending on \( u \). Virtual & magnified when \( u < f \).
• Concave Lens: Always virtual, erect & diminished (\( v = -\text{ve} \), \( m < 1 \)).

POWER OF LENS & COMBINATION OF THIN LENSES

⚡ Power of a Lens (P)

\( P = \dfrac{1}{f(\text{metres})} = \dfrac{100}{f(\text{cm})} \)
SI Unit: Dioptre (D) (1 D = 1 m⁻¹)
Convex lens: \( P > 0 \) | Concave lens: \( P < 0 \)

🔗 Combination of Lenses in Contact

\( P_{\text{net}} = P_1 + P_2 + P_3 + \dots \)
\( \dfrac{1}{f_{\text{net}}} = \dfrac{1}{f_1} + \dfrac{1}{f_2} + \dfrac{1}{f_3} \)
Total Magnification: \( m_{\text{net}} = m_1 \times m_2 \times m_3 \)
