Chapter 8 Notes

Predicting What Comes Next: Exploring Sequences and Progressions

1. Sequences and Patterns

A sequence is an ordered list of numbers following a definite rule or pattern. Each number in the sequence is called a term ($a_1, a_2, a_3, \dots, a_n$).

Arithmetic Progression (AP)

An Arithmetic Progression is a sequence of numbers such that the difference between any two consecutive terms is constant.

This constant difference is called the common difference ($d$).

$$\mathbf{d = a_{k+1} - a_k}$$

2. General Term of an AP ($n^{\text{th}}$ Term)

If $a$ is the first term and $d$ is the common difference, the terms of the AP are:

$$a, \; a+d, \; a+2d, \; a+3d, \; \dots$$
$$\mathbf{a_n = a + (n - 1)d}$$

where $a_n$ is the $n^{\text{th}}$ term, $a$ is the first term, $n$ is the term number, and $d$ is the common difference.

3. Sum of First $n$ Terms of an AP

Sum Formulas

The sum $S_n$ of the first $n$ terms of an AP is given by:

$$\mathbf{S_n = \frac{n}{2} \left[ 2a + (n - 1)d \right]}$$

Alternatively, if the last term $l$ ($a_n$) is known:

$$\mathbf{S_n = \frac{n}{2} (a + l)}$$

4. Solved Examples

Example 1: Finding the 15th Term of an AP

Question: Find the $15^{\text{th}}$ term of the AP: $3, 7, 11, 15, \dots$

Solution:

First term $a = 3$, Common difference $d = 7 - 3 = 4$, Term number $n = 15$.

$$a_{15} = a + (15 - 1)d = 3 + 14(4) = 3 + 56 = 59$$
Example 2: Sum of First 20 Natural Numbers

Question: Calculate the sum of the first $20$ terms of the sequence $1, 2, 3, 4, \dots, 20$.

Solution: Here $a = 1, l = 20, n = 20$.

$$S_{20} = \frac{20}{2} (1 + 20) = 10 \times 21 = 210$$

5. Summary

Quick Checklist
  • The common difference $d$ can be positive, negative, or zero.
  • If $d > 0$, the AP is increasing; if $d < 0$, the AP is decreasing.
