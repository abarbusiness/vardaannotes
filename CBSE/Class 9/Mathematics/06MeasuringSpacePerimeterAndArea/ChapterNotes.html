Measuring Space: Perimeter and Area

1. Area of Triangles & Heron's Formula

When the base and height of a triangle are known:

$$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Base} \times \text{Height}$$

When all three sides $a, b, c$ of a triangle are known, we use Heron's Formula:

$$\mathbf{\text{Area} = \sqrt{s(s - a)(s - b)(s - c)}}$$

where $s$ is the semi-perimeter:

$$\mathbf{s = \frac{a + b + c}{2}}$$

2. Area of Special Polygons & Quadrilaterals

Formula Collection
  • Equilateral Triangle: $\text{Area} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} a^2$
  • Isosceles Triangle: $\text{Area} = \frac{b}{4} \sqrt{4a^2 - b^2}$ (where $a$ is equal side, $b$ is base)
  • Rhombus: $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times d_1 \times d_2$
  • Trapezium: $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2} \times (\text{Sum of parallel sides}) \times \text{Height}$

3. Circle Area & Perimeter

Circular Geometry
  • Circumference of Circle: $C = 2\pi r = \pi d$
  • Area of Circle: $A = \pi r^2$
  • Area of Sector: $A_{\text{sector}} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2$
  • Arc Length: $L = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times 2\pi r$

4. Solved Examples

Example 1: Applying Heron's Formula

Question: Find the area of a triangle whose sides are $13\text{ cm}$, $14\text{ cm}$, and $15\text{ cm}$.

Solution:

1. Calculate semi-perimeter $s$:

$$s = \frac{13 + 14 + 15}{2} = \frac{42}{2} = 21\text{ cm}$$

2. Calculate area:

$$\text{Area} = \sqrt{21(21-13)(21-14)(21-15)} = \sqrt{21 \times 8 \times 7 \times 6}$$ $$\text{Area} = \sqrt{(7 \times 3) \times (2^3) \times 7 \times (2 \times 3)} = \sqrt{7^2 \times 3^2 \times 2^4} = 7 \times 3 \times 4 = 84\text{ cm}^2$$
Example 2: Area of a Sector

Question: Find the area of a sector of a circle with radius $6\text{ cm}$ if angle of the sector is $60^\circ$. ($\pi = \frac{22}{7}$)

Solution:

$$\text{Area} = \frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \pi \times 6^2 = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 36 = \frac{132}{7} \approx 18.86\text{ cm}^2$$

5. Summary

Quick Summary
  • Ensure all dimensions are in the same unit before calculating area or perimeter.
  • Semi-perimeter is half of the total perimeter.
