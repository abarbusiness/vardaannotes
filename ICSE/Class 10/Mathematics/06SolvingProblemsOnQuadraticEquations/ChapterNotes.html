Chapter 6: Solving Problems on Quadratic Equations

Word problems test your ability to translate real-life situations into mathematical equations. In this chapter, we master the 3-step solution framework across 7 major categories of word problems, enforcing root rejection rules for practical constraints.

1. General Methodology & 3-Step Strategy

3-Step Problem Solving Framework
  1. Step 1 (Variable Assignment): Express the unknown quantity of the problem as variable '$x$'.
  2. Step 2 (Equation Formulation): Translate word conditions into an algebraic equation in '$x$' and reduce to standard form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$.
  3. Step 3 (Solving & Root Selection): Solve for $x$ and reject extraneous/inadmissible roots based on physical realities.
Inadmissible Root Rejection Rules
  • Lengths / Widths / Heights: Must be strictly positive ($x > 0$).
  • Time / Distance / Speed: Must be strictly positive ($x > 0$).
  • Number of Persons / Items / Tiles / Rows: Must be a positive integer ($x \in \mathbb{N}$).
  • Ages: Must be positive ($x > 0$).

2. Category 1: Problems Based on Numbers, Digits & Fractions

Practice Problems — Numbers & Digits
Q1. Find two natural numbers which differ by 3 and the sum of whose squares is 117.
Solution:
Let smaller natural number = $x$, larger = $x+3$.
$x^2 + (x+3)^2 = 117 \Rightarrow x^2 + x^2 + 6x + 9 = 117 \Rightarrow 2x^2 + 6x - 108 = 0$
$x^2 + 3x - 54 = 0 \Rightarrow (x+9)(x-6) = 0 \Rightarrow x = -9$ or $x = 6$.
Reject $x = -9$ as $x \in \mathbb{N}$. So $x = 6$, and other number = $6+3 = 9$.
Ans: 6 and 9
Q2. The sum of two natural numbers is 8 and the difference of their reciprocals is $\frac{2}{15}$. Find the numbers. [ICSE 2015]
Solution:
Let numbers be $x$ and $8-x$.
$\frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{8-x} = \frac{2}{15} \Rightarrow \frac{8-2x}{8x-x^2} = \frac{2}{15} \Rightarrow \frac{4-x}{8x-x^2} = \frac{1}{15}$
$15(4-x) = 8x - x^2 \Rightarrow 60 - 15x = 8x - x^2 \Rightarrow x^2 - 23x + 60 = 0$
$(x-20)(x-3) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 3$ (since $x < 8$). Other number = $8-3 = 5$.
Ans: 3 and 5
Q3. A two-digit number is such that product of its digits is 6. When 9 is added to the number, digits interchange places. Find the number. [ICSE 2014]
Solution:
Tens digit = $x$, unit digit = $y = \frac{6}{x}$. Number = $10x + \frac{6}{x}$.
Reversed number = $10\left(\frac{6}{x}\right) + x = \frac{60}{x} + x$.
$\left(10x + \frac{6}{x}\right) + 9 = \frac{60}{x} + x \Rightarrow 9x + 9 = \frac{54}{x} \Rightarrow x + 1 = \frac{6}{x}$
$x^2 + x - 6 = 0 \Rightarrow (x+3)(x-2) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 2$ (digit cannot be negative).
Unit digit $y = \frac{6}{2} = 3$. Original number = $10(2) + 3 = 23$.
Ans: 23

3. Category 2: Problems Based on Time & Work / Pipes & Cisterns

$$\text{Combined 1-Hour Work Rate: } \frac{1}{\text{Total Time}} = \frac{1}{\text{Time}_A} + \frac{1}{\text{Time}_B}$$
Practice Problems — Time & Work
Q1. $A$ takes 6 hours less than $B$ to complete a work. Together they finish it in 13 hours 20 minutes. Find time $B$ takes alone.
Solution:
Let $B$'s time = $x$ hrs $\Rightarrow A$'s time = $(x-6)$ hrs. Combined time = $13\frac{1}{3} = \frac{40}{3}$ hrs.
$\frac{1}{x-6} + \frac{1}{x} = \frac{3}{40} \Rightarrow \frac{2x-6}{x^2-6x} = \frac{3}{40} \Rightarrow 80x - 240 = 3x^2 - 18x$
$3x^2 - 98x + 240 = 0 \Rightarrow (x-30)(3x-8) = 0$.
If $x = \frac{8}{3}$, $A$'s time = $\frac{8}{3}-6 < 0$ (impossible). So $x = 30$.
Ans: 30 hours

4. Category 3: Problems Based on Geometrical Figures & Measurement

Geometric Relations
  • Right Triangle: $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ (Pythagoras Theorem)
  • Uniform Border / Footpath: Outer Area $(L \times B)$ $-$ Inner Area $[(L-2x)(B-2x)] = \text{Path Area}$
Practice Problems — Geometry & Measurement
Q1. Hypotenuse of a right triangle is 13 cm and difference between other two sides is 7 cm. Find the sides.
Solution:
Let shorter side = $x$, longer side = $x+7$.
$x^2 + (x+7)^2 = 13^2 \Rightarrow x^2 + x^2 + 14x + 49 = 169 \Rightarrow 2x^2 + 14x - 120 = 0$
$x^2 + 7x - 60 = 0 \Rightarrow (x+12)(x-5) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 5$ cm.
Longer side = $5+7 = 12$ cm.
Ans: 5 cm and 12 cm
Q2. A footpath of uniform width runs inside a field $32\text{ m} \times 24\text{ m}$. If path area is $208\text{ m}^2$, find width.
Solution:
Outer area = $32 \times 24 = 768\text{ m}^2$. Inner area = $(32-2x)(24-2x)$.
$768 - (32-2x)(24-2x) = 208 \Rightarrow 112x - 4x^2 = 208 \Rightarrow x^2 - 28x + 52 = 0$
$(x-26)(x-2) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 2$ m (since $x=26$ exceeds field breadth).
Ans: 2 m

5. Category 4: Problems Based on Distance, Speed & Time

$$\text{Speed Increase } (\Delta v): \quad \frac{D}{v} - \frac{D}{v + \Delta v} = \text{Time Saved } (\Delta t)$$
$$\text{Upstream / Downstream Flow: } \text{Upstream } = v - x, \quad \text{Downstream } = v + x$$
Practice Problems — Speed & Distance
Q1. Increasing car speed by 10 km/h reduces journey time for 72 km by 36 mins. Find original speed. [ICSE 2005]
Solution:
$\frac{72}{x} - \frac{72}{x+10} = \frac{36}{60} = \frac{3}{5} \Rightarrow \frac{720}{x^2+10x} = \frac{3}{5} \Rightarrow \frac{240}{x^2+10x} = \frac{1}{5}$
$x^2 + 10x - 1200 = 0 \Rightarrow (x+40)(x-30) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 30$ km/h.
Ans: 30 km/h
Q2. Motorboat speed in still water is 9 km/h. It goes 12 km downstream and back in 3 hours. Find stream speed.
Solution:
Downstream speed = $9+x$, Upstream = $9-x$.
$\frac{12}{9+x} + \frac{12}{9-x} = 3 \Rightarrow \frac{12(9-x+9+x)}{81-x^2} = 3 \Rightarrow \frac{216}{81-x^2} = 3$
$72 = 81 - x^2 \Rightarrow x^2 = 9 \Rightarrow x = 3$ km/h.
Ans: 3 km/h

6. Category 5: Commercial, Cost Price & Financial Problems

Practice Problems — Financial & Budget Problems
Q1. Selling an article for ₹24, trader loses as much percent as cost price. Calculate cost price.
Solution:
C.P. = ₹$x$, Loss % = $x\% \Rightarrow \text{S.P.} = x - \frac{x^2}{100} = 24$
$x^2 - 100x + 2400 = 0 \Rightarrow (x-60)(x-40) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 60$ or $x = 40$.
Ans: ₹60 or ₹40
Q2. Food budget for picnic was ₹480. Eight students failed to join, so share of each increased by ₹10. How many went? [ICSE 2008]
Solution:
Original students = $x$, actual = $x-8$.
$\frac{480}{x-8} - \frac{480}{x} = 10 \Rightarrow \frac{3840}{x^2-8x} = 10 \Rightarrow x^2 - 8x - 384 = 0$
$(x-24)(x+16) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 24$. Actual attendees = $24 - 8 = 16$.
Ans: 16 students

7. Category 6: Problems Based on Ages

Example: 5 years ago a woman's age was square of her son's age. 10 years hence her age will be twice son's age. Find present age of woman. [ICSE 2007]
Solution:
Let son's age 5 yrs ago = $x \Rightarrow$ woman's age 5 yrs ago = $x^2$.
Son's age 10 yrs hence = $x+15$, woman's age 10 yrs hence = $x^2+15$.
$x^2 + 15 = 2(x+15) \Rightarrow x^2 - 2x - 15 = 0 \Rightarrow (x-5)(x+3) = 0 \Rightarrow x = 5$.
Woman's present age = $x^2 + 5 = 5^2 + 5 = 30$ years.
Ans: 30 years

Tips for ICSE Board Exams
  1. Always write down variable definitions explicitly (e.g. "Let original speed of car be $x$ km/h").
  2. Double-check unit conversions (e.g. 20 minutes = $\frac{20}{60} = \frac{1}{3}$ hour).
  3. State explicit reasoning when rejecting a root (e.g., "Since speed cannot be negative, $x = -40$ is rejected").
  4. Re-read the question prompt to answer the final requirement (e.g. if asked for "students who actually attended", answer $x-8$, not just $x$).
