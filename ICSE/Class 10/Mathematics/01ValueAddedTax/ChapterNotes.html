Value Added Tax / GST

ICSE Class 10 Mathematics • Chapter 01

1. Introduction to Indirect Taxation (VAT & GST)

Value Added Tax (VAT) / GST: Indirect taxes levied on the sale of goods and services at each stage of the supply chain.

Key Terminology

Term Meaning
Listed Price / Marked Price (M.P.) Original price printed on product by manufacturer
Discount Reduction given on marked price (always on M.P.)
Sale Price / Cost Price Price after discount, before adding tax
Bill Amount / Invoice Amount Final amount = Sale Price + Tax

2. Basic Calculations

Sale Price (SP): $\text{SP} = \text{Marked Price} - \text{Discount}$

Tax Amount: $\text{Tax} = \frac{\text{Tax Rate}}{100} \times \text{SP}$

Bill Amount: $\text{Bill} = \text{SP} + \text{Tax}$
